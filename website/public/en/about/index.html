TELLURIS / About Project Uriel

ABOUT THE TEAM · PROJECT URIEL

Curiosity drives us.
Evidence guides us.

We are Project Uriel, an independent multidisciplinary initiative from Costa Rica. We created TELLURIS to turn Earth-system questions into understandable, verifiable and useful investigations.

01The team

The people and skills behind TELLURIS

Our work brings together software, integrations, automation, and technical electronics and electrical expertise. We collaborate to turn complex data into accessible tools without claiming to be an official NASA organization or mission.

01 / SOFTWARE & INTEGRATIONS

SOFTWARE & INTEGRATIONS

Web development, APIs, data processing, interoperability and applications connecting services with real-world use.

02 / AUTOMATION & SYSTEMS

AUTOMATION & SYSTEMS

Reproducible workflows, technical validation, traceability, deployment and monitoring.

03 / ELECTRONICS & ELECTRICAL WORK

ELECTRONICS & ELECTRICAL WORK

Technical expertise related to physical systems, electronics and the electrical field.

04 / SCIENTIFIC COMMUNICATION

SCIENTIFIC COMMUNICATION

Visualizations, documentation and explanations that show methods, sources, uncertainties and limitations.

02Our principles

How we work

We start from explicit questions, use accessible NASA public datasets where supported and keep code, sources and conclusions separate. Automated tests do not replace scientific validation or human review.

PROJECT URIEL · 2026

Our participation

TELLURIS was built for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, «Be An Earth System Trend Detective!». Project Uriel is an independent participating team, not a NASA division.

SCIENTIFIC STANDARD

A broad catalog is not 49 working integrations.

Every source needs a specific mission or provider, exact collection, dates, resolution, units, missing-data rules, quality masks, statistical checks and license review. Event catalogs—such as earthquakes—require methods different from climate time series.

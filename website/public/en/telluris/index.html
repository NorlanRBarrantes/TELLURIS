01 / THE PROBLEM

Having data is not understanding Earth.

A graph can reveal a trend without telling us whether it reflects natural variability, a physical change, or limitations in the source. We need to ask, compare, and verify.

02The methodology

From original data to
traceable interpretation.

TELLURIS neither fabricates measurements nor declares automatic conclusions. Every step preserves the origin of the evidence.

  1. 01

    Select

    Choose a geographic point, valid observation years and documented variables.

  2. 02

    Retrieve

    Query the NASA POWER API and expose the original source and units.

  3. 03

    Check

    Require complete months, identify missing periods and respect each data product.

  4. 04

    Analyze

    Estimate Theil–Sen slopes and matched-year differences; disclose statistical assumptions.

  5. 05

    Explain

    Translate the results into clear language, including uncertainty and limitations.

04Scientific rigor

Precision starts with
knowing what we know.

TELLURIS separates a geographic observation, a statistical estimate and a scientifically validated conclusion. They are not interchangeable.

TRACEABLE DATA

Source matters

NASA POWER provides gridded reanalysis/satellite products—not sensors installed at the selected coordinates. NASA GIBS imagery on the globe visualizes the Earth, not a historical trend measurement.

EXPLICIT UNCERTAINTY

Uncertainty matters

Theil–Sen slopes are descriptive estimates. p-values and intervals depend on independence assumptions; co-moving variables do not establish causation.

  • Annual series require all 12 months to be valid.
  • The 1985–2025 period is used where supported; albedo begins in 2001 for the verified locations.
  • Two-site comparisons align calendar years and preserve original units.

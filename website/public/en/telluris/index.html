THE CHALLENGE

Four questions. One transparent investigation.

The 2026 NASA Space Apps challenge calls for a way to identify environmental variables, visualize their time series and assess trend magnitude and statistical significance.

01

WHAT?

Choose a well-defined variable, such as near-surface air temperature or precipitation.

02

WHERE?

Specify the selected point, coordinates and underlying spatial footprint.

03

HOW MUCH?

Estimate direction and magnitude with clearly stated units and uncertainty.

04

SIGNIFICANT?

Use a justified test, report assumptions and avoid confusing significance with causation.

Sources and scientific boundaries

The candidate provider is NASA POWER. Planned variables include T2M and PRECTOTCORR. TELLURIS will distinguish gridded point estimates from regional observations and statistical significance from causal explanation.

Translate scientific terms into plain language
Theil–Sen
Median slope of the annual observations; not a prediction.
p-value
Conditional on statistical assumptions; not proof of climate significance or cause.
Confidence interval
Its interpretation depends on assumptions, especially independence.
Paired calendar years
Two sites are compared only for years present in both sources.
NASA POWER point
Gridded model/satellite product, not an instrument installed at that coordinate.

Source integrity: NASA POWER supplies original monthly observations grouped by RE/AG community (maximum 20 parameters per request). Annual aggregations are deterministic. Comparative trends here are descriptive, unadjusted for serial dependence and multiple testing; co-occurrence is not causation. 04 / INTERPRETATION

What the numbers do not prove

Statistical significance is not yet established for serially dependent climate observations. The original Mann–Kendall p-value is shown for audit, not as a validated conclusion.

NASA POWER data are gridded reanalysis or satellite-derived products, not measurements made exactly at the selected coordinates. Trends require homogeneity checks, serial-dependence review and independent comparison.

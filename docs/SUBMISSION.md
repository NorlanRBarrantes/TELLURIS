## Suggested narrative for reviewers (draft structure, not results)

1. **Problem:** Earth system trends are hard to find, interpret, and validate.
2. **Approach:** A guided investigation that uses NASA observations, time-series analysis and significance testing.
3. **Scientific integrity:** Transparent units, time windows, test assumptions, and evidence provenance.
4. **Demo:** A genuine and reproducible investigation (to be recorded after it works).
5. **Impact:** A reusable approach for science education and accessible environmental evidence.

Avoid numerical claims unless they are generated by the validated system and backed by a reproducible analysis.

## Reproducible quality-control demonstration — MODIS coastal-source error, 2026-11-15

One defensible NASA Space Apps narrative is: the original Limón point passed as a climate model location but failed MODIS land-water QA because the satellite's chosen 250 m grid pixel is shoreline/shallow-ocean coded. **Preserve that negative result.** In response the team pre-registered mainland candidates and screened them **blind to NDVI/EVI trend** with original NASA MOD13Q1 quality flags and annual 500 m MCD12Q1 IGBP classification, documented in [EXP-003C](EXP-003C-SPATIAL-PRESCREEN.md) and [EXP-003D](EXP-003D-IGBP.md). NASA audit runs [#43743357320](https://github.com/NorlanRBarrantes/TELLURIS/actions/runs/43743357320) and [#43743662878](https://github.com/NorlanRBarrantes/TELLURIS/actions/runs/43743662878) passed 9 + 8 respective unit tests. **Avoid** implying the new candidate is a verified protected rainforest over 26 years or a live public MODIS trend feature; it is a **provisional quality-controlled research site**.

## EXP-005 — NASA fourth objective: statistical evidence, not overclaiming

TELLURIS visibly reports a Theil–Sen trend per decade, an independent-year rank-based 95% confidence interval, original Mann–Kendall p, lag-1 residual diagnostic, two exploratory stationary-block sensitivity p-values and early/late trend sensitivity. A new JSON report preserves source metadata, selected variable and annual observations. A region comparison can assess **paired year-by-year differences**, without claiming a causal mechanism or representative countrywide result. **Six numerical SciPy source-reference comparisons passed**: [#43755159596](https://github.com/NorlanRBarrantes/TELLURIS/actions/runs/43755159596). 66/66 website tests passed [#43755613674](https://github.com/NorlanRBarrantes/TELLURIS/actions/runs/43755613674). Neither p < .05 nor CI excluding zero is presented as **independently validated climate significance** without product homogeneity/stationarity verification. [Full method](EXP-005-SIGNIFICANCE.md).
